# PXD007535 licensing evidence

## Sources

- PRIDE data policy: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/markdownpage/datapolicy
- EMBL-EBI terms of use: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/about/terms-of-use/
- PXD007535 PRIDE record: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD007535

## Finding

The PRIDE data policy states that datasets submitted from June 2018 are covered by CC0, while datasets submitted before June 2018 are subject to the EMBL-EBI terms of use. The PXD007535 record reports the original submission/request history beginning in 2017 and an original announcement in 2018; the PRIDE API metadata lists an updated project metadata date in 2024.

The applicable dataset-level rule is therefore the pre-June-2018 EMBL-EBI terms-of-use path, not the post-June-2018 CC0 rule. The EMBL-EBI terms state that EMBL-EBI itself places no additional restrictions on use or redistribution beyond restrictions provided by original data owners, unless otherwise specified in the terms.

This resolves the repository-policy question but does not by itself prove that the original data owners impose no additional restrictions, nor does it establish that all acquired files may be redistributed. OysterBox may retain local copies for the controlled audit, but the public repository will publish only metadata, manifests, checksums, and provenance notes.
