# PXD007535 MCP participant-artifact notes

## Source

Molecular & Cellular Proteomics publisher route: https://www.mcponline.org/article/S1535-9476(20)32223-4/fulltext

## Evidence

The publisher full-text extract states that the demographic and clinical details of participants are presented in supplementary File S1.

The same methods section states that the discovery phase used whole saliva from 67 systemically healthy subjects, divided into four groups: generalized chronic periodontitis (CP), generalized aggressive periodontitis (AP), gingivitis (G), and periodontal health (H). The article gives 17 individuals for AP, 17 for CP, and 17 for G; it describes the healthy group but the exact healthy count is not included in the saved excerpt.

The publisher article also states that complete medical and dental histories were obtained, clinical periodontal indices were recorded, and written consent covered use of saliva samples and clinical data. These are participant-level study descriptions, not a sample-to-subject mapping for the selected technical sample `20151204_022_SP61`.

## Governance conclusion

Supplementary File S1 is the next candidate artifact for metadata-only review. It may contain group-level demographic/clinical data, but it must not be assumed to contain a pseudonymous identifier linking `20151204_022_SP61` to a participant. The file should be inspected only for workbook structure, column names, row counts, pseudonymous IDs, group labels, and sample-link fields. Measurement values, validation outcomes, biomarker results, and clinical outcome values must remain outside the reconciliation workspace.
