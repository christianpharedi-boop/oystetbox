
## PXD033169 candidate lead

Source: https://proteomecentral.proteomexchange.org/cgi/GetDataset?ID=PXD033169

ProteomeXchange describes PXD033169 as a peer-reviewed original PRIDE dataset titled “Novel Diagnostic Biomarkers for High-Grade Serous Ovarian Cancer Uncovered by Data-Independent Acquisition Mass Spectrometry.” The public record states that discovery used DIA-MS on depleted serum from 26 HGSOC cases and 24 healthy controls, and that 18 of 28 prioritized candidates were validated using targeted MS assays in an independent cohort. It identifies PRIDE as the hosting repository, provides a public FTP route, records announcement on 2023-11-14, and links the 2022 publication by Huh et al.

This is a strong provenance-first candidate lead because it explicitly distinguishes discovery and independent targeted validation at cohort level and has a public accession. It is not yet admitted: sample-to-subject mapping, exact validation sample manifest, discovery cutoff, artifact hashes, outcome sealing, licensing/access terms, and processing completeness still require metadata-only verification.
